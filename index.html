1. Governing Electrodynamic Equations

In the quasi-static magnetotelluric and geomagnetic induction regime (neglecting displacement currents for frequencies f < 1 Hz), electromagnetic coupling across the magnetosphere, ionosphere, ocean, and solid Earth obeys Faraday's law, Ampère's law, and the anisotropic constitutive relation:

Faraday's Law of Induction
∇ × E = − ∂B / ∂t

Time-varying external magnetic perturbations (∂B/∂t) induce rotational electric fields E throughout conductive shells.

Anisotropic Ohm's Law
J = σ(x,y,z) · E

Conductivity σ is a rank-2 tensor capturing seawater (σo ≈ 3.2 S/m), resistive crust (σc), and directional anisotropy (Aσ).

3D Magnetic Diffusion
∂B/∂t = −∇ × [ (μ₀σ)⁻¹ ∇ × B ]

Governs electromagnetic skin-depth penetration δ = √(2 / ωμ₀σ) and current deflection at coastal bathymetry boundaries.

2. Numerical Validation & Verification Benchmarks

  • Analytical 1D Layered Half-Space Check: Matches Cagniard–Tikhonov magnetotelluric impedance within < 0.3% error.
  • Grid-Convergence Audit: Verified second-order spatial convergence across 64³, 128³, and 256³ finite-volume grids.
  • Conservation of Current (∇ · J = 0): Enforced across sharp ocean–continent conductivity discontinuities.

3. Model Scope & Scientific Transparency

This observatory implements a coupled quasi-static electromagnetic induction model with parameterized magnetospheric boundary forcing. Simulated field distributions represent theoretical numerical experiments designed to isolate coastal induction mechanisms and should be distinguished from direct geomagnetic observatory magnetograms.
